## 🌟 Overview

CyberAware is a modern cybersecurity awareness web application developed to help users understand common cyber threats, online safety practices, and digital security concepts.

As digital technology becomes an essential part of daily life, cyberattacks such as phishing, social engineering, malware infections, and data breaches continue to increase. CyberAware aims to bridge the cybersecurity knowledge gap by providing accessible, engaging, and easy-to-understand educational content for users of all backgrounds.

The platform promotes safer online behavior and encourages individuals to develop strong cyber hygiene habits in today's digital world.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Millions of internet users face cybersecurity risks every day due to a lack of awareness and proper digital safety education.

Common threats include:

- 🎣 Phishing attacks
- 🕵️ Social engineering scams
- 🔐 Weak password practices
- 🦠 Malware and ransomware infections
- 📊 Data privacy breaches
- 🌐 Unsafe browsing habits

Many users are unaware of how these threats work and how to protect themselves effectively.

CyberAware was created to address this challenge by delivering cybersecurity knowledge through an intuitive and user-friendly platform.

## 🏆 Hackathon Journey

CyberAware was developed during **Indus Week 2026**, a premier innovation and technology event hosted by **Sukkur IBA University**.

The project addresses one of the most pressing challenges of the digital age: the lack of cybersecurity awareness among everyday internet users. Despite significant advancements in security technologies, human error remains a major factor behind successful cyberattacks, including phishing, social engineering, malware infections, and data breaches.

Our team set out to create a platform that transforms cybersecurity education into an accessible and engaging experience. By combining modern web technologies with intuitive design principles, CyberAware enables users to better understand online threats and adopt safer digital practices.

Developed within the fast-paced environment of a hackathon, the project demonstrates our commitment to leveraging technology for social impact while showcasing practical problem-solving, collaboration, and modern software development practices.

CyberAware represents our vision of a safer digital future where awareness and education serve as the first line of defense against cyber threats.

## 🌍 Why CyberAware?

Cybersecurity awareness is one of the most effective defenses against cybercrime.

While advanced security technologies continue to evolve, human error remains one of the leading causes of successful cyberattacks. Educating users about online threats can significantly reduce risks and improve digital safety.

CyberAware focuses on empowering users through knowledge, helping them recognize threats and make informed decisions online.
